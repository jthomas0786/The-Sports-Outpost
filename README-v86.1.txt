The Sports Outpost — v86.1 NFL Weekly Slate + Props + Grades Hotfix

What changed
- NFL product boundary is Tuesday through Monday.
- New weekly slate rolls early Tuesday morning; Sunday refresh remains inside the same week.
- TD Feed persists across Thursday/Sunday/Monday and resets only when the Tuesday slateId changes.
- Full weekly player pool is eligible for every prop board even when sportsbook lines are not posted yet.
- Missing simulation probability is treated as missing, never as 0%.
- Yardage/volume grades now support A+, A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C.
- Weekly TD Feed ordering is chronological across separate games.
- Simulation cache/state receives the weekly slate key and rolls cleanly with the Tuesday slate.

Important
Sportsbook lines can still appear later in the week as books post them. A missing price no longer hides the modeled player.
