TSO v88.6e — Player + Field Overlay Concept Match

This pass targets the two pieces that still looked furthest from the approved
NFL Gamecast concept: the live field players and the broadcast field overlay.

PLAYER REBUILD
- Removes the short chibi/cartoon-face field actors.
- Replaces them with taller adult-proportioned football silhouettes.
- Adds separate QB, offensive-line, skill-player and defender poses.
- Faces offense and defense toward each other according to possession.
- Keeps team-color uniforms and can use the existing team logo URL on helmets.
- Adds the concept-style electric-blue focus ring under the QB and target.
- Rebuilds route ghosts as neutral white/gray silhouettes.
- Uses perspective scale from the same field coordinate map.

FIELD OVERLAY REBUILD
- Line of scrimmage = layered cyan beam: soft halo + medium glow + bright core.
- Line to gain = matching layered yellow beam.
- Both down lines now render below the player layer instead of cutting through bodies.
- Pass path is a thin cyan broadcast arc.
- Route continuation is a thin white/cool-blue line with arrow head.
- Live ball marker is football-shaped, with seam/laces, not a circular dot.
- Subtle alternating turf bands were added without changing field geometry.

UNCHANGED / PRESERVED
- 120-yard regulation coordinate model: 10-yard EZ + 100-yard field + 10-yard EZ.
- 10 20 30 40 50 40 30 20 10 labels.
- Concept-derived stadium/crowd/light shell.

- Existing real player headshot flow to Current Play / Featured Player.
